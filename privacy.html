Privacy policy

Who uses it: one person, the owner of the Gmail account. The app is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: the owner's own Gmail messages, with the read-only scope gmail.readonly. It cannot send, delete or change mail, labels or settings.

How the data is used: to sort and summarise the owner's mail for the owner.

Where the data goes: it is processed on the owner's own devices. To sort and summarise it, parts of it may be sent to an AI service the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude), under the owner's own account. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion: the access token is stored locally on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com, under Security, Third-party apps.
